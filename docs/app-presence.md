# App-wide presence setup

Online indicators use private Supabase Realtime Presence, scoped to each 1:1 conversation. Users share presence only when the app is visible and Share presence is enabled on that device. A green dot means the other user's presence is currently visible; “offline or not sharing” means no presence is visible, not proof that the person is actually offline. Backgrounding, connectivity loss, disabled sharing, browser suspension, or delays can all affect it. The two users need the updated app. Multiple open devices are supported: any visible device sharing presence keeps the user visible.

## Deployment

1. Back up the database and apply `docs/migrations/014_app_presence.sql` in staging after the base conversation schema. Ensure Supabase Realtime and private channel authorization are enabled. This migration adds SELECT and INSERT policies on `realtime.messages` for presence only, restricted to authenticated conversation members. Review **all existing permissive realtime.messages policies**: they combine with OR and could grant broader access. The app does not switch off public Realtime channels globally, as other existing features use them.
2. Test with two conversation members and a third unrelated account. Verify the third cannot join or track the private `app-presence:<conversation UUID>` topic, and that authenticated members can both read and publish presence. Also verify access after a conversation is deleted, reconnects and token refresh. Existing broad policies must be removed or tightened before relying on this boundary.
3. Deploy the app and test the inbox and chat header across two devices: active anywhere in the app, background/foreground, sharing off/on, multiple tabs, reconnect, sign-out, and new/deleted chats. A missing policy, disabled Realtime, revoked membership, or failed connection can leave presence unavailable; the UI does not fall back to public channels or claim the partner is offline.

Realtime presence is best effort, not an identity-verification mechanism: an authorized member controls their own client and presence key, and can spoof a key. It should not be used for security or to infer intent. Old device-local last-seen timestamps may remain after sharing is turned off. App-wide tracking maintains one Realtime channel per conversation; projects with many chats should monitor connection/channel quotas. Older app versions do not publish the new app-wide presence until updated.
